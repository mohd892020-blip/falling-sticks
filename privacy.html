Privacy Policy

Stickbolt, formerly called Falling Sticks (Android app and website) · Version 1.1.2 · Effective date: November 12, 2026

The short version

Stickbolt has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell or share data. Your progress stays on your own device. The only thing that goes online is the online High Scores: the nickname you choose, your best score and an anonymous player ID. Nothing else.

What is stored, and where

  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, items, achievements, stickers, settings, save backups) and the optional player name and pet name you type are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage (the browser's local storage on the website).
  • We, the developer, cannot see this data and do not receive a copy of it.
  • Apart from the online High Scores (see below), it leaves your device only if you choose to copy an export code (Settings › Data › Export).
  • If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is a feature of Android, controlled by you in your phone's settings and handled by Google.

Network and permissions

  • The Android app contains the whole game and works offline. The only data it sends is your High Scores entry (see below), and only when you are online.
  • Permissions: vibration (short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings) and internet, used to send and show the online High Scores and to open links you tap in your browser.
  • The website loads the game files from GitHub Pages (our host), which, like any web host, may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Google Play

  • The Android app uses no third-party SDKs except Google's Google Play In-App Review library. After some level wins it may ask Google Play to show its rating card. That card is shown and handled by the Google Play Store app; any rating or review you write there goes to Google, not to us.
  • Google Play (downloads, updates, reviews) and Android system components such as the web view are operated by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Online High Scores

  • When you finish an Endless run or the daily challenge with a new personal best, the game sends that entry to the online High Scores: the nickname you choose (up to 12 letters or numbers), your score, the level you reached in Endless, and an anonymous player ID.
  • The player ID is a random code made by Google Firebase Authentication when the game first goes online. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account, and we cannot use it to find out who you are.
  • Nicknames and scores are public: every player can see them in the High Scores. Please don't use your real full name. Names with bad words are refused.
  • The High Scores are stored by Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), which processes the data for us under Firebase's privacy and security terms. Like any server, Google may keep technical logs such as IP addresses for a short time.
  • The daily challenge list starts fresh every day. Your Endless entry stays until you ask us to remove it.
  • You can play without any of this: if you skip the name, nothing is sent.

Children

Stickbolt has no ads, no chat, no sign-in and no way to contact other players. The only thing other players can see is the nickname and score on the online High Scores, and names with bad words are refused. Parents who want an entry removed, or who have questions, can contact us at the address below.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Data › Reset progress starts the game over. A few save backups are kept on the device so a reset can be undone.
  • To erase everything, including those backups: on Android, open your phone's Settings › Apps › Stickbolt › Storage › Clear storage (or uninstall the app). On the website, clear the site data for this site in your browser.
  • To remove your High Scores entry, email us your nickname and score (and roughly when you played). We delete it within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts to collect or send any other data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
