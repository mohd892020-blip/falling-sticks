Privacy Policy

Falling Sticks (Android app and website) · Version 2.11 · Effective date: November 8, 2026

The short version

Falling Sticks does not collect, sell or share personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your own device.

What is stored, and where

  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, items, achievements, stickers, settings, save backups) and the optional player name and pet name you type are saved only on your device, in the app's local storage (the browser's local storage on the website).
  • We, the developer, cannot see this data and do not receive a copy of it.
  • It leaves your device only if you choose to: by copying an export code (Settings › Data › Export) or by sending a challenge link (see below).
  • If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's saved progress in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is a feature of Android, controlled by you in your phone's settings and handled by Google.

Network and permissions

  • The Android app contains the whole game and works offline. The game itself does not upload or send your data anywhere.
  • Permissions: vibration (short haptic feedback, which you can turn off in Settings) and internet, which Android's built-in web view needs to display the game's built-in pages and which lets links you tap open in your browser.
  • The website loads the game files from GitHub Pages (our host), which, like any web host, may keep standard technical logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Google Play

  • The Android app uses no third-party SDKs except Google's Google Play In-App Review library. After some level wins it may ask Google Play to show its rating card. That card is shown and handled by the Google Play Store app; any rating or review you write there goes to Google, not to us.
  • Google Play (downloads, updates, reviews) and Android system components such as the web view are operated by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Challenge links

When you challenge a friend, the game builds a link that contains only the game mode, the level, a random game seed (so your friend gets the same round), your score and the nickname you chose. Nothing else is in it. The link is sent only to the people you pick, through the app you pick (for example a messaging app), and that app's own privacy policy applies.

Children

Falling Sticks has no ads, no chat, no accounts and no way to contact other players. Because the game does not collect personal information, it does not collect personal information from children either. Leaderboard rivals are clearly labelled bots, not real people. Parents who have questions can contact us at the address below.

Deleting your data

  • Settings › Data › Reset progress starts the game over. A few save backups are kept on the device so a reset can be undone.
  • To erase everything, including those backups: on Android, open your phone's Settings › Apps › Falling Sticks › Storage › Clear storage (or uninstall the app). On the website, clear the site data for this site in your browser.
  • Since we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts to collect or send data (for example an online leaderboard), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
