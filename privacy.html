Privacy Policy

Falling Sticks (Android app and website) · Last updated: November 6, 2026

The short version

Falling Sticks does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored, and where

  • Your progress (levels, stars, coins, items, settings, the optional player and pet names you type) is saved only on your own device, in the app's local storage.
  • This data never leaves your device unless you choose to export it (Settings › Data › Export) or share a challenge link.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Network and permissions

  • The Android app contains the whole game and works completely offline. It does not download, upload or send anything.
  • Its permissions are vibration (short haptic feedback you can turn off in Settings) and internet, which Android's built-in web view needs to display the game. The game itself sends nothing.
  • If you share a challenge link, the link contains your chosen name, the level and your score, and it is sent only to the people you choose, through the app you pick.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because nothing is collected, no personal information from children is gathered or shared.
